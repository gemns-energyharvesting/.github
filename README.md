## 🧠 About GEMNS

GEMNS is a deep-tech engineering organization specializing in **low-power wireless systems** and **connected embedded devices**.

We design and build production-grade solutions across:
- **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**
- **Z-Wave (including Long Range)**
- **Zigbee & Green Power**
- **Custom RF / Sub-GHz systems**

From silicon to cloud — we deliver complete, optimized systems.

## 📶 Core Expertise

### 🔹 Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
- Custom advertising protocols (encrypted manufacturer data)
- GATT design & optimization
- Extended vs Legacy Advertising tuning
- Ultra-low power beacon design
- Mobile integration (iOS / Android)

### 🔹 Z-Wave
- Long Range (LR) device development
- Wake-On-Event End Nodes (WOEEN)
- S2 security & nonce handling optimization
- Controller + End device integration
- Energy optimization for batteryless / energy-harvesting nodes

### 🔹 Zigbee
- Zigbee 3.0 device development
- Green Power (GPD) devices
- Custom clusters & command handling
- Interoperability with ecosystems (e.g., Philips Hue)

### 🔹 Ultra-Low Power Systems
- Energy harvesting devices (event-driven wakeup)
- Sub-mJ boot optimization
- Deep sleep and cold boot strategies
- TX/RX energy profiling & optimization
